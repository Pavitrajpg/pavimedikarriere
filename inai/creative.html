What We Offer

Six creative capabilities.
One integrated practice.

Every creative service at INAI is connected to a strategic outcome. Nothing is decorative for its own sake.

Brand Identity

Your brand is not your logo. It is the total impression your business makes on every person who encounters it. We build complete visual identities that hold together across every touchpoint, from business card to billboard, from website to social media story.

Includes: logo design, colour system, typography, icon set, brand guidelines document, usage rules
Visual Design

Design that does a job. Packaging, marketing materials, digital assets, print collateral. Every design decision is made with the audience in mind and the brand guidelines as the constraint. The result is visual work that feels consistent and intentional.

Includes: print collateral, digital graphics, poster and banner design, brand asset library
Social Media Design

A social media presence that actually looks like one business, not a collection of individually designed posts. We build template systems, content frameworks, and campaign assets that keep your feed consistent and your brand recognisable at a scroll.

Includes: post templates, story templates, highlight covers, profile setup, content calendar design
Content Strategy

What to say, when to say it, and how to say it so the right people actually pay attention. A content strategy is the architecture underneath the posts, the articles, and the campaigns. Without it, you are producing content. With it, you are building an audience.

Includes: audience mapping, content pillar framework, posting schedule, tone of voice guide, content calendar
Marketing Collateral

The materials that represent you when you are not in the room. Brochures, pitch documents, one-pagers, capability statements. Designed to the standard of the business you are presenting to, not the standard of what the template allows.

Includes: brochures, flyers, capability decks, sales one-pagers, event materials, exhibition graphics
Corporate Presentations

Investor decks, client proposals, board presentations, team briefings. A presentation is a persuasion tool. We design them to move the audience from where they are to where you need them to be, with structure, clarity, and visual authority.

Includes: pitch deck design, proposal templates, keynote and PowerPoint master slides, presentation coaching

The INAI Approach

Creative work is
strategic work.

There is a version of creative work that is about aesthetic preference. A slightly different shade of blue. A font that feels more premium. A logo that is rounder or sharper. That version of creative work produces beautiful things that do not move the business forward.

At INAI Creative, every creative decision is connected to a strategic question. Who needs to understand something, and what do we need them to understand? What does trust look like in this industry? What does the brand need to communicate at first glance to the right person?

The kolam is the metaphor. Every line is intentional. Every dot is connected. The beauty is a consequence of the structure, not a substitute for it.

"Creative work that does not serve a strategic purpose is decoration. INAI Creative builds brands that work."
