Content Strategy

A content calendar is not a content strategy. A content strategy is the architecture that decides what you say, who you say it to, on which channel, in what format, and towards which business outcome. The calendar is the execution. The strategy is what makes the calendar worth building.

Includes: content pillar framework, audience-channel mapping, content calendar, format guidelines, performance metrics
