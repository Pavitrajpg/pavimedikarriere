How we work

Five stages, used as needed.

Engagements are adaptable. Not every project needs all five stages. Some begin at Define because the problem is already clear, and some end at Diagnose.

INAI is a founder-led studio. Where a project calls for specialist skills, we collaborate with trusted professionals.

Discover

Understand the organisation and its objectives.

2

Diagnose

Identify underlying problems and opportunities.

3

5

Implement & Evolve

Support adoption, evaluate outcomes and improve.

Ways to work with us

Start small. Go as far as it makes sense.

There is no fixed menu and no rate card. Most engagements take one of three shapes, and we shape the scope around your problem, not the other way round.

Shape 01

Starting Point

A focused look.

For when something is not working and you are not sure what, or you want a second view before committing to anything.

  • A conversation and review of how things work today
  • A written summary of what we found
  • A clear recommendation on what to do next, including when the answer is not yet
Mostly Advisory
Shape 02

Focused Build

One thing, built properly.

For a defined need, such as a website, a CRM, a portal, a workflow, a brand system or a working prototype.

  • Scoping and design with you and your team
  • Build and testing in the open, with regular check-ins
  • Documentation and handover, so it is yours to run
Advisory + Innovation
Shape 03

Ongoing Partnership

Someone alongside you as things evolve.

For organisations that want continued thinking and building as priorities change, not a one-off project.

  • Regular reviews of what is working and what is not
  • Improvements and new builds as needs shift
  • Training and enablement for your team
Advisory + Innovation

Scope and investment are agreed together, in advance, for each engagement. Not every project fits neatly into one of these shapes, and that is fine.
