Case Study

Step4Progress: From zero systems
to a functioning operation.

The situation. Step4Progress is a UK-affiliated study abroad consultancy with offices across India, UK, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. When INAI began working with them, there was no CRM, no structured lead pipeline, no content strategy, and no digital infrastructure. Everything was manual.

The build. A full-stack CRM was built from scratch using Supabase and deployed on GitHub Pages as a single HTML file. Features included role-based access control, India view-only enforcement, a 645-university course finder with AI suggestions via Anthropic API, lead management with bulk upload, Twilio call recording via Edge Functions, a student portal with animated lesson delivery, and Meta and GA4 analytics integration.

The content. Simultaneously, Instagram was grown from 300 to 1,030 followers in 10 days through a content strategy built around student success stories, destination education guides, and community engagement.

The result. A business that had no digital infrastructure now has a complete operational backbone, a growing social presence, and a content library of premium downloadable guides for lead generation.
